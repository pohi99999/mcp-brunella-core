# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- **Tone**: Professional and Direct.
- **Language**: Documentation and user-facing outputs should be primarily in Hungarian, while the codebase remains in English.

## UX Principles
- **Robustness First (Zero Broken Windows)**: Prioritize error-free execution and robust error handling across the Dashboard and CLI.

## Agent Behavior
- **Stop & Fix (EPP Protocol)**: Agents must halt immediately upon encountering errors and require a fix before proceeding to ensure precision.
