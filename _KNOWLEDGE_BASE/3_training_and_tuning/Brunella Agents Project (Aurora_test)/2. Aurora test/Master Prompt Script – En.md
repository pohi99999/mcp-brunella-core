CLI Piping & Redirection, CLI Virtual Environments, Meta-Prompting, and Iterative Refinement.

Follow these phases in order, documenting your reasoning and outputs at each step:

PHASE 1 – DATA RECOVERY & FILTERING

PHASE 2 – PROBLEM DIAGNOSIS
- Use Few-Shot Prompting with 3 provided examples to identify root causes.
- Apply Chain-of-Thought reasoning to map the causal chain of failures.

PHASE 3 – STRATEGIC PLANNING
- Use Tree-of-Thought to generate at least 3 alternative recovery strategies.
- Apply Meta-Prompting to create the optimal prompt for selecting the best strategy.

PHASE 4 – COMMUNICATION ADAPTATION
- Present the chosen strategy in three Role-Based formats:
   1. Engineer (technical depth)
   2. Project Manager (timeline & resources)
   3. Investor (business value & ROI)

PHASE 5 – QUALITY ASSURANCE
- Apply Self-Critique Loop to review the entire output.
- Use Iterative Refinement to improve clarity, accuracy, and impact.

CONSTRAINTS:
- Maintain clear, structured documentation.
- Explicitly label each phase and technique used.
- Highlight reasoning steps and decision points.
- Ensure outputs are actionable and ready for integration.

FINAL DELIVERABLE:
A comprehensive, multi-section report containing:
1. Executive Summary
2. Detailed Phase Outputs
